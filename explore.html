2. Fair-Play, Anti-Cheat & AI Referee Verification
No third-party injectors, aim-assists, GFX hacks, macros, teaming, or emulator bypasses on mobile lobbies. Scoreboards undergo Tournyx AI Referee & human audit. Violations cause immediate disqualification, prize forfeiture, and permanent hardware/account ban.
3. 15-Minute Check-In, Room ID & Slot Discipline
Room ID & Password unlock 15 minutes before start inside Room & Schedule and via Push Notification. Teams must occupy their exact assigned Slot # at least 5 minutes before launch. Sharing Room IDs with unregistered outsiders leads to instant ban.
4. Ticket-ID Escrow, Disconnects & Instant Payouts
Paid entries verify your exact TX Pass code server-side with a permanent receipt saved in Tickets. Individual lag or battery disconnects do not qualify for rematches unless the game server crashes for the entire lobby. Verified winnings settle directly to Tournyx Wallets.
5. DPDP Act (India) Privacy & Player Data Protection
In compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, Tournyx collects only essential tournament data (IGN, Game UID, Email/Phone for verification & payouts, and match proof screenshots). Your private contact details are never sold to third parties, and you may request data review or deletion anytime via Profile Support.
