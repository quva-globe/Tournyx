Resolution & Payout FAQ

1. How fast are winner payouts & alerts sent?
Instantly. Finalizing runs an atomic PostgreSQL transaction that credits winning captains' wallets, unlocks official E-Certificates, and sends instant OneSignal Push + Email notifications even if players have the app closed.
2. How does 1-Click AI Score Import work?
If you uploaded match screenshots in Host Studio → AI Brackets, tap "Import AI Scores" to automatically match squad names, fill kills/points, and pre-assign podium ranks. You can still edit any row before finalizing.
3. How are Earnings (₹) & Points (PTS) deducted?
Cash prizes deduct from your verified Earnings balance; Point prizes deduct from your Points balance. The server validates balances with row locks to prevent double deductions.
4. How are E-Certificates issued?
Eligible squads (Champions, Top 3 & MVP, or All Participants) automatically receive unique verifiable CERT-XXXX codes. You can also attach a custom-designed certificate image/PDF per squad before finalizing.
5. Can I edit payouts after finalizing?
No. Once finalized, the match locks as VERIFIED and funds transfer immediately. Always verify the bottom Total Deduct bar and proof screenshots before confirming.
6. Can I reward both Cash and Points to a squad?
Yes! Open "Custom / Cert" on any squad card to add bonus Cash (₹), Points (PTS), or a custom certificate on top of podium rewards.
7. Why is the Finalize button disabled?
It locks automatically if your wallet balance is below the total payout or if mandatory podium ranks (e.g., 1st/2nd/3rd when prize tiers exist) haven't been assigned yet.
